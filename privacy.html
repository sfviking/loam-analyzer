Loam Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 August 2026

Loam records and analyzes your sport — mountain biking, road cycling, running, walking, weightlifting, golf — using your iPhone, Apple Watch and AirPods. The analysis happens on your device. There is no Loam account, no Loam server, and no Loam database. We do not have a copy of your rides, and we could not look at them if we wanted to.

This policy covers the Loam Capture iOS app (including Loam Dev and Loam VIP builds) and the Loam web analyzer at sfviking.github.io/loam-analyzer.

The short version

  • Your recordings stay on your device unless you choose to send them somewhere.
  • We run no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising, and there is no third-party SDK in the app collecting anything. The only crash reporting is Apple's own: TestFlight builds send crash logs and any feedback you submit to Apple and on to us; App Store builds share crash logs only if you opted in to sharing analytics with developers in iOS Settings.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your data. There is nobody to share it with.
  • Three features send your athletic data off your device, and all three are things you turn on: iCloud sync, Strava, and the AI coach. Two ordinary web requests happen on their own: a weather lookup for a recording's conditions, and map tiles when you open a map (both listed below).
  • Deleting the app deletes your data with it.

What Loam records

When you start a recording, and only while a recording is running, Loam captures from the sensors you have enabled:

  • Motion — accelerometer, gyroscope and device attitude from the iPhone, from AirPods (head motion), and from an Apple Watch (wrist motion), plus barometric altitude, step and activity data.
  • Location — GPS position, speed and altitude, including in the background so capture continues with the screen locked.
  • Heart rate, when an Apple Watch is worn.
  • Labels you tap — segment starts, lift sets, club types, crash marks and similar annotations.
  • Video you choose to attach to a golf swing. Video is used only to draw the swing view; it is held in memory, never uploaded, and never saved with the session.

Loam does not record audio, does not read your contacts, photos (beyond saving a chart when you ask it to), messages, or anything else on your device.

Where it is stored

Recordings are written to the app's own storage on your iPhone. They stay there until you delete them, or until you delete the app.

If you enable iCloud sync, Loam mirrors your recordings into its own iCloud Drive container so they are backed up and available across your devices. That is your iCloud account, governed by Apple's privacy policy. Loam has no access to it.

Apple Health

With your permission, Loam reads heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, VO₂ max, workout and activity data, and swim workouts (lengths, strokes and open-water routes) from Apple Health. It uses them to add heart rate to a recording, to show your recovery and readiness trends, and to analyze a swim. A pool swim can also be recorded by Loam itself (phone on the deck, Apple Watch on your wrist), but the imported Apple Watch swim is the better read, so both routes exist and the import leads.

The iPhone app writes nothing to Apple Health. The Apple Watch app records a standard workout session (which is what lets it read heart rate and keep capturing with your wrist down) and saves that workout to your Health app, the same as any other workout app.

Health data is read on your device and stays on it, except in two cases you control: if you use the AI coach, a summary of your vitals may be included in what is sent to Anthropic (see The AI coach); and if you turn on iCloud backup, the heart-rate file saved with a recording (from your watch, or imported from Health for that session) is mirrored to your own iCloud Drive with the recording. No other Health data is copied anywhere.

Strava

Connecting Strava is optional, and everything else in Loam works without it.

When you connect, Loam asks Strava for permission to:

PermissionWhat Loam does with it
activity:writeUpload a recording you choose to upload, and set its sport type and your default gear.
read, read_allRead your name, and your starred segments (including private ones) so a ride can be compared against your own PRs.
activity:read_allRead your own activities, segment efforts and best efforts, for the stats and progression views.
profile:read_allRead your bikes and shoes, so uploads can carry the right gear.

Strava data is requested by your phone, shown only to you, and cached on your device so the app opens quickly and works offline (and, if you turn on iCloud backup, mirrored to your own iCloud Drive; disconnecting deletes both copies). Loam only ever accesses your own Strava data. It never requests, displays, or stores another athlete's data, and it never aggregates data across users — there is no server on which that could happen.

When you disconnect Strava in Settings, Loam revokes its access with Strava and deletes every local copy of your Strava data in the same action: your stats, activity history, best efforts, starred segments and gear. Revoking Loam's access from your Strava settings page has the same effect on Strava's side.

The AI coach

The AI coach is optional and off until you use it. When you ask it a question, your device sends a compact summary of the session to Anthropic's API (api.anthropic.com), which generates the coaching text. This is the one feature that sends your athletic data to a company that is not Apple or Strava, so it is worth being precise about what goes:

  • A summary of the analyzed session — the computed metrics (turns, cadence, reps, tempo and so on), not the raw sensor stream.
  • If you have granted Health access, a summary of your Apple Health vitals: readiness, heart rate variability against your baseline, resting heart rate, training load and VO₂ max.
  • If you have connected Strava, a short block of your recent Strava activity and segment efforts.
  • Whatever you type into the coach.

Anthropic processes it under their own terms and privacy policy. Loam does not store your conversations anywhere but on your device, and nothing you send is used to train any model by us.

In the web analyzer you supply your own Anthropic API key, and the request goes from your browser to Anthropic directly. In the app, the coach may run on the developer's own Anthropic account so you do not need a key of your own (with a daily limit per device); you can enter your own key instead in the coach's settings, and it then takes precedence.

If you would rather nothing left your device, simply do not use the coach. Every other analysis in Loam is computed locally and works without it.

The web analyzer

The analyzer at sfviking.github.io/loam-analyzer is a single web page. When you drop a recording onto it, the file is read and analyzed inside your browser. It is never uploaded. There is no server to upload it to.

Two things about any web page still apply, and are worth saying plainly:

  • Map tiles. Showing your route on a map means requesting map images for the area you rode from a tile provider (Esri/ArcGIS, CARTO, or OpenTopoMap, depending on the style chosen). Those requests necessarily tell that provider roughly where the route is, along with your IP address.
  • Page assets. The page loads its charting and mapping libraries and its fonts from public CDNs (unpkg, cdnjs, plot.ly, Google Fonts), which receive your IP address as any web request does. The copy of the analyzer bundled inside the iOS app loads its libraries locally instead.

Your settings and saved sessions in the web analyzer are stored in your browser's own local storage, on your machine. Clearing your browser data clears them.

Weather

The app can show conditions for a recording using Apple's WeatherKit, which involves sending the location and time of the recording to Apple to get the forecast for that place. Apple's privacy policy governs that request.

Your rights and choices

  • Access and portability. Your data is already in your hands: recordings export as CSV, GPX and TCX from the app.
  • Deletion. Delete a session in the app to remove it. Delete the app to remove everything. Disconnect Strava to erase every local copy of your Strava data.
  • Withdrawing permission. iOS Settings controls Loam's access to location, motion and Health at any time. Strava access is revocable from Strava.
  • GDPR / UK GDPR. Where those apply, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Because Loam holds no copy of your data, most of these you exercise directly on your own device, immediately and without asking us. For anything you cannot do yourself, contact us below.

Children

Loam is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the previous versions remain visible in the project's history. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
